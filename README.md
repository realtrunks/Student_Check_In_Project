## Program Description

In my project, I will develop a student-check-in kiosk program that is designated to have students check in at departments/locations of a high school. The goal of this program is to create an efficient check-in process for students checking into different departments of the school, track attendance by eliminating truancies causing unexcused absences or tardies, and create reports of student check-in activities that include attendance trends and reasons for visits. The departments will include a guidance office, attendance office, library, college and career center, and nurse's office. My program will require students of a local high school to check in on a computer-kiosk that askes for the student's ID, student's first and last name, and where they are checking into. The kiosk will notify the student they have successfully checked into the specific department they are at. Once they are finished visiting that department, they will need to check out. Once the student enters their student ID, the system will inform the student that are checked out. Users of the program such as teachers and administrative staff will have access to how long students are at specific departments for and reason for visits. I am holding myself accountable in completing this project by May 18th, 2024. 
I intend on breaking down my project using what I learned from SDLC. Week 1 will be focusing on gathering all my requirements and the feasibility of my project. Week 2 will focus on the design of my project. Week 3-4 will focus on the software development of my project. Week 5-6 will focus on testing my project ensuring that my software program runs to the exact needs of my goals and users. Week 7 will focus on deployment of my project.
